Nesaron Page Saver Privacy Policy

Version 1.10, last updated 13 November 2026.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Nesaron Page Saver, buy a licence or contact us. It covers the browser extension in every edition, our website at www.nesaron.com, licence purchases and support.

  • Nesaron Page Saver collects no personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Everything you save goes to a folder on your own device. We never receive, see or keep your pages, files, videos or browsing history.
  • The only time the extension contacts anyone other than the websites you save is when it checks a licence key with our licence provider, Lemon Squeezy. It sends the key and a device label, nothing else.
  • Machine-made captions are optional and off until you turn them on. They use your browser's own speech recognition on your device only. Nesaron Page Saver sends no sound and no text anywhere, and we never receive them. The first time you use a language, your browser may download its speech-recognition language pack from the browser's maker.
  • Your highlights, underlines and notes stay with you. They are kept inside your saved file on your device and, until you save them into the file, in your browser's own storage on your device. Nesaron Page Saver sends them nowhere and we never receive them.
  • Quick PDF uses your browser's own PDF engine, on your device. For this, your browser shows at install that Nesaron Page Saver can access the “page debugger backend”. Nesaron Page Saver uses that only when you click Quick PDF, only on that tab and only for the few seconds it takes. Nothing it reads leaves your device.
  • When you buy a licence, Lemon Squeezy is the seller. It shares your name, email address and order details with us so we can supply your licence and support.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we never will.

2. What stays on your device

Nesaron Page Saver works inside your own browser. When you click Save, it reads the page you are viewing (and, if you save several pages, the pages you choose), builds the saved file in your browser, and writes it to your Downloads folder or to a folder you choose. The documents, video, audio and captions you choose to keep go to the same place.

To work, the extension keeps these on your device, in your browser's own extension storage:

  • your settings;
  • your Library list: for each saved item, its title, web address, date, file name, the details its citation is made from (such as the author, website name and publication date) and up to 300,000 characters of its text, so you can search it and copy its citation;
  • citation details you corrected for a web page, by its web address, so your correction is used when you copy a citation or save that page (kept until you undo the correction or uninstall Nesaron Page Saver; the 300 most recent are kept);
  • a reference to the folder you chose for saving, if you use that option, and references to any other folders you allow so your notes can be saved into books you moved (you can remove them in Settings);
  • your licence details, if you have a licence: the licence key, the activation reference, the date it was last checked, and the name and email address that Lemon Squeezy links to your licence, so Settings can show whose licence is active;
  • how many free tries of the paid feature you have used;
  • the date and version of the terms you accepted, and of the video agreement if you turned on video saving;
  • if you use machine-made captions: that you confirmed how they work (and when), the language you chose and your caption settings; and
  • until your browser closes: where your browser saves downloads, whether it asks where to save each file, and the saved books for which you chose “Not now” when asked to allow a folder.

Highlights, underlines and notes. Saved pages and books include tools to mark words and write notes. What you mark and write is kept inside that saved file. Until it is in the file, a copy is kept in your browser's own storage for files opened from your device, so nothing is lost if the tab closes; it is removed once the notes are in the file. If you allow Nesaron Page Saver to access files opened from your device (a switch in your browser's extension settings that stays off until you turn it on), a small part of Nesaron Page Saver looks at each file you open from your device only to see whether it is a Nesaron Page Saver file, and does nothing on any other file. On a Nesaron Page Saver file, a moment after you change your notes, and when you press Save notes, Nesaron Page Saver reads the saved file you have open, checks that it is the same file, puts your notes into its notes part and writes it back to the same place. It does this only for the file you are viewing, and only where your browser lets it write: in your browser's download folder (to find that folder it writes a tiny file there once each time your browser starts, and removes it at once), in the folder you chose for saving, or in a folder you allow in Nesaron Page Saver's own window, which opens when a book is somewhere it cannot write. Without the switch, your browser shows its own file window and you choose the file; Nesaron Page Saver can write only to the file you choose, and changes only the notes part of it. Browsers that can do neither keep your notes in their own storage on your device. The extension does not keep your notes or anything it read from the file. Citation details you correct inside a saved page or book are kept in exactly the same way as your notes; where a saved file has no notes tools, they stay in your browser's own storage for files opened from your device.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete items from the Library at any time. Uninstalling Nesaron Page Saver deletes all of it from your browser. The files you saved stay on your device until you delete them.

3. What the extension does not collect

Nesaron Page Saver does not collect, record or send us:

  • your name, email address or any other personal information (the licence details described above stay on your device);
  • the pages, files, videos or text you save;
  • the sound of your videos, or the words of captions and transcripts made from them;
  • your highlights, underlines and notes, or the words you mark;
  • your browsing history, the websites you visit or what you search for;
  • usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or device identifiers; or

It contains no advertising, no trackers and no code downloaded after installation. It does not set cookies.

4. When the extension connects to the internet

Nesaron Page Saver makes only two kinds of connection:

  • The websites you choose to save. To save a page, your browser fetches its pictures, styles, fonts and, if you choose, its attached documents and media, from that website, using your own browser session. This is the same as viewing the page. Those websites may see the request and set their own cookies under their own privacy policies.
  • Our licence provider, Lemon Squeezy. When you activate, check or deactivate a licence key, the extension sends Lemon Squeezy the licence key, an activation reference and a device label made of your browser type and the activation date. While a licence is active, it checks the key about every 14 days when you are online. Like any internet request, Lemon Squeezy can see your IP address. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy covers that data.

Nothing else. Free users who never enter a licence key make no connection other than to the websites they save.

Highlights, underlines and notes

These tools make no connection at all. They are part of the saved file itself and work offline, one-click Save notes works entirely on your device, and the notes never leave your device unless you share the file yourself. Notes go wherever the file goes, so remove any you don't want others to see before you share a file.

Quick PDF (exact look)

Quick PDF makes no connection. In browsers that allow it, when you click Quick PDF, Nesaron Page Saver uses the permission your browser describes as access to the “page debugger backend” to ask your browser to draw the PDF of that one page with the browser's own PDF engine. It uses this permission only for Quick PDF, only on the tab you clicked it in, and only until the PDF is made, a few seconds. In that time it reads the page's size and the page itself, as the browser draws it, and briefly shows the page with its screen look, loads its waiting pictures and opens out inner scroll areas; it then puts the page back as it was. The PDF goes to your Downloads folder like any other save. Nesaron Page Saver does not use this permission on any other tab, does not record what you do, and sends nothing to us or anyone else. Your browser shows its own notice while this happens.

Machine-made captions

If you turn on machine-made captions, Nesaron Page Saver plays the sound of a video you saved silently inside its own browser tab and asks your browser to write down the words using speech recognition that runs on your device. It always asks for on-device recognition only, and never uses your browser's online speech service. If your browser cannot recognise speech on your device, nothing runs. The sound and the words never leave your device. Nesaron Page Saver makes no connection for this feature, and we never receive the sound, the words or any information about what you transcribe.

The first time you choose a language, your browser may download a speech-recognition language pack for that language from the browser's maker, as it does for other parts of the browser. That download is made by your browser, not by Nesaron Page Saver, and is covered by your browser maker's own privacy policy. Like any download, it lets the browser maker see your IP address. Nesaron Page Saver tells you this before you turn the feature on, and asks you to confirm before it is used.
