Version 1.7, last updated 5 November 2026.
Applies to Page Saver Pro 2.23.0 and later.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Page Saver Pro, buy a licence or contact us. It covers the browser extension in every edition, our website, licence purchases and support.

  • Page Saver Pro collects no personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Everything you save goes to a folder on your own device. We never receive, see or keep your pages, files, videos or browsing history.
  • The only time the extension contacts anyone other than the websites you save is when it checks a licence key with our licence provider, Lemon Squeezy. It sends the key and a device label, nothing else.
  • Machine-made captions are optional and off until you turn them on. They use your browser's own speech recognition on your device only. Page Saver Pro sends no sound and no text anywhere, and we never receive them.
  • Your highlights, underlines and notes stay with you. They are kept inside your saved file on your device.
  • We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, and we never will.

2. What stays on your device

Page Saver Pro works inside your own browser. When you click Save, it reads the page you are viewing (and, if you save several pages, the pages you choose), builds the saved file in your browser, and writes it to your Downloads folder or to a folder you choose. The documents, video, audio and captions you choose to keep go to the same place.

To work, the extension keeps these on your device, in your browser's own extension storage:

  • your settings;
  • your Library list: for each saved item, its title, web address, date, file name and up to 300,000 characters of its text, so you can search it;
  • a reference to the folder you chose for saving, if you use that option;
  • your licence details, if you have a licence;
  • how many free tries of the paid feature you have used;
  • the date and version of the terms you accepted;
  • if you use machine-made captions: that you confirmed how they work, the language you chose and your caption settings.

Highlights, underlines and notes

Saved pages and books include tools to mark words and write notes. What you mark and write is kept inside that saved file when you press Save notes. Until then, a copy is kept in your browser's own storage for files opened from your device, so nothing is lost if the tab closes. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete items from the Library at any time. Uninstalling Page Saver Pro deletes all of it from your browser.

3. What the extension does not collect

Page Saver Pro does not collect, record or send us:

  • your name, email address or any other personal information;
  • the pages, files, videos or text you save;
  • the sound of your videos, or the words of captions and transcripts made from them;
  • your highlights, underlines and notes, or the words you mark;
  • your browsing history, the websites you visit or what you search for;
  • usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or device identifiers; or
  • your location.

It contains no advertising, no trackers and no code downloaded after installation. It does not set cookies.

4. When the extension connects to the internet

Page Saver Pro makes only two kinds of connection:

  • The websites you choose to save. To save a page, your browser fetches its pictures, styles, fonts and, if you choose, its attached documents and media, from that website, using your own browser session.
  • Our licence provider, Lemon Squeezy. When you activate, check or deactivate a licence key, the extension sends Lemon Squeezy the licence key, an activation reference and a device label. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy covers that data.

Machine-made captions

If you turn on machine-made captions, Page Saver Pro plays the sound of a video you saved silently inside its own browser tab and asks your browser to write down the words using speech recognition that runs on your device. It always asks for on-device recognition only, and never uses your browser's online speech service. Page Saver Pro makes no connection for this feature, and we never receive the sound or the words.
