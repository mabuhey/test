Your own offline library, made from the web.

More than a "Save As" button. Nesaron Page Saver follows a site's own contents, saves the pages in order and puts them together as one clean book you can search.

Save hundreds or thousands of pages as one book.

When the site has its own table of contents, it reads it and keeps the pages in that order, in a contents tree you can fold. Search every page at once. If your computer runs low on memory, it stops early and keeps what it has saved. You can also make one PDF of the whole book, with contents, bookmarks and page numbers.

  • Big books open quickly
  • Carry on a save that stopped
  • Or the whole site as a ZIP

Videos on the page saved with it, plus captions.

Most videos* and audio that play inside the page are saved with it and play offline. If a video has no captions, you can make them in English, on computers where Chrome supports on-device speech recognition. It listens at normal speed, so a one-hour video takes about an hour. Captions are machine-made, so they may contain mistakes.

  • Streaming video saved as MP4
  • Nothing is uploaded

Highlight, add notes and cite.

Highlight in 6 colours, underline and write notes. After a one-time setup step, your notes save into the file itself with no save window, so they travel with the file. If you move a book to a new folder, it asks once which folder the book is in. Every saved page comes with draft citations you can check, correct and copy.

  • APA 7, MLA 9, Harvard, Chicago 17, AGLC4
  • Notes listed in the contents

Markdown for your own AI tools.

Turn a saved book into Markdown files for the AI tool or note app you already use. It reads the pages, the attached PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files, and any video captions, then splits big books into parts that stay under common upload limits. It all happens on your computer.

  • Reads the text inside attached files
  • Each part says which one comes next

Questions and answers

Nesaron Page Saver reads your saved book, the PDF, Word, PowerPoint and Excel files saved with it, and any video captions, and writes them as Markdown files. A big book is split into parts that stay under common upload limits, and each part names the next one. You then add the files to the AI tool or note app you use. A scanned PDF that is only a picture of the page has no text for it to read. Nesaron Page Saver itself sends nothing to any AI service.
Yes, most of them, on pages you can open yourself, including when you are signed in. Streaming video is saved as an MP4 file. It never clicks Start, Submit or Reply buttons. It is designed not to save protected or subscription streaming video, live streams or videos from video-sharing sites. Save only what you have the right to keep.
A wider kind of multi-page save. Instead of one book, it saves many pages of a website into one ZIP file, with a list of every page saved and a short read-me. It uses the same safety rules as a book: it skips sign-out, delete and checkout links and pauses politely between pages.
You pay once and use the paid features on one computer, with no renewal fee. Need it on a second computer? Buy a second licence, or use the yearly plan on that computer. The Licence Agreement has the full terms.

Into the saved file on your computer. The first time you use it, a short setup asks you to turn on Chrome's "Allow access to file URLs" switch for the extension. After that, notes save themselves into the file about a second after each change. If you send the file to someone, your notes go with it.
The PDF uses the standard fonts built into every PDF reader, so it shows text in the Latin alphabet, such as English, Filipino, Spanish or French. Other writing systems, such as Chinese, Arabic or Thai, show as question marks in the PDF. The saved book itself shows every language as normal.
